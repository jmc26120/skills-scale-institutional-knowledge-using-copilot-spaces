# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Program Sponsors

### Role Summary
Program Sponsors set strategic direction, approve major investments, and ensure projects stay aligned to business goals and priorities. They provide executive sponsorship and help resolve cross-functional conflicts when priorities compete.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic goals and outcomes for the program or initiative
- Approve scope, budget, and priority trade-offs
- Sponsor key decisions that affect multiple teams or workstreams
- Align project outcomes with broader portfolio and business objectives
- Support escalations when risks or dependencies require executive intervention

### Goals
- Ensure strategic alignment and organizational value
- Support successful delivery of high-impact initiatives
- Reduce ambiguity in decision-making and ownership

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates and steering committee reviews
- Portfolio or program prioritization discussions
- Escalation of major risks, scope changes, or cross-team blockers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on status, milestone decisions, and risk escalation
- Collaborates with Product Managers on priority alignment and business value
- Provides direction to leaders and stakeholders when trade-offs affect scope, timeline, or resources
- Partners with Delivery Managers and Team Leads to unblock dependencies and remove organizational obstacles

---

## Delivery Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate execution across teams, track delivery health, and help remove blockers that could slow progress. They focus on flow, dependency management, and operational execution.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate delivery across multiple teams or workstreams
- Monitor plans, milestones, and dependencies
- Identify bottlenecks, risk areas, and cross-team blockers
- Drive operational rhythm for planning, review, and follow-through
- Support team alignment on delivery commitments and trade-offs

### Goals
- Maintain predictable execution and throughput
- Improve dependency visibility and issue resolution
- Help teams deliver value without unnecessary friction

### Typical Communication
- Cross-team status reviews and dependency discussions
- Delivery health reports and milestone check-ins
- Escalation paths for blockers, staffing issues, or schedule pressure

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to maintain planning accuracy and reporting
- Coordinates with Developers, Team Leads, and Product Managers to manage scope, priorities, and risk
- Acts as the operational link between team execution and broader stakeholder communication
- Helps Product Managers and Project Managers understand delivery constraints before commitments are made

---

## Product Owners / Business Representatives

### Role Summary
Product Owners or Business Representatives translate business needs into clear priorities and validate that delivered outcomes meet stakeholder expectations. They are accountable for value realization and product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Prioritize features and work based on business value and customer need
- Clarify requirements, acceptance criteria, and desired outcomes
- Partner with stakeholders to refine scope and opportunities
- Validate solutions against business goals and user needs
- Help resolve competing priorities and decisions during delivery

### Goals
- Maximize customer and business value
- Keep delivery focused on the highest-priority outcomes
- Facilitate clear decision-making for product direction

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and prioritization discussions
- Product reviews, stakeholder feedback sessions, and release readiness checks
- Acceptance criteria and feature validation conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define strategy and market alignment
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to ensure features meet expected value and quality
- Provides context to Project Managers and Delivery Managers for planning and milestone decisions
- Helps resolve ambiguity between stakeholder expectations and implementation realities

---

## Security and Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners ensure projects follow required controls, policies, and risk standards. They help teams integrate security reviews, compliance requirements, and governance checkpoints into the delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Review delivery plans against security and compliance requirements
- Identify control gaps, risks, and required remediation
- Support secure design decisions and policy adherence
- Coordinate approvals, audits, and risk reviews
- Help teams understand governance expectations and review timelines

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Ensure project work meets required controls and standards
- Support secure, auditable delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and checkpoint meetings
- Compliance risk assessments and control discussions
- Approval workflows and governance follow-ups

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to integrate review gates into milestones
- Advises Developers and Product Managers on constraints, approvals, and secure-by-design practices
- Coordinates with Sponsors when significant governance or policy issues affect scope or deadlines
- Works with QA and Release Managers to confirm release readiness and reduce operational risk

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, deployment timing, communications, and rollback planning. They help ensure that software is delivered in a controlled, well-understood way.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, sequencing, and communication
- Confirm deployment readiness and quality checkpoints
- Manage stakeholder communication and escalation during releases
- Support rollback readiness and contingency planning
- Align release timing with business or operational constraints

### Goals
- Deliver changes with minimal disruption
- Improve predictability and traceability in deployment activities
- Reduce release-related risks and communication gaps

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Go/no-go decisions and release announcements
- Post-release communication and issue tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to align release timing with milestones
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to confirm readiness and quality
- Coordinates with Product Owners and Sponsors on business impact and communication
- Interfaces with Security and Compliance Partners to confirm control and approval requirements before launch

---

## Quality Assurance / Quality Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance representatives define quality expectations and validate that work meets standards before release. They ensure quality is designed into the process, not only checked at the end.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality criteria, testing approaches, and acceptance validation
- Create and review test plans, regression coverage, and quality gates
- Identify defects, quality risks, and release-readiness issues
- Partner with teams on continuous quality improvement
- Support confidence in delivery decisions and release readiness

### Goals
- Reduce defects and rework
- Ensure delivery quality aligns with customer expectations
- Raise confidence in release readiness and operational stability

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and quality review meetings
- Defect triage and release readiness discussions
- Risk-based quality assessments and quality gate decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to ensure testability and quality standards are built into delivery
- Collaborates with Product Owners to validate that outcomes meet business expectations
- Supports Project Managers and Delivery Managers with risk-informed readiness assessments
- Coordinates with Release Managers to confirm critical quality checks are complete before deployment
